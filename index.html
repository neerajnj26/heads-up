How to Play Heads Up!

1

Pick a Category & Hold on Forehead

Select any deck, tap I'm Ready!, and hold your phone landscape on your forehead with the screen facing your friends.

2

Friends Give Clues

Your friends act, hum, describe, or shout clues without saying any part of the word on screen.

3

Tilt to Score or Pass

Tilt phone face-down when you guess right. Tilt phone face-up to pass. Bring it back level to load the next word.

Troubleshooting & Desktop Controls:

• iPhone users: Tap "Allow" when prompted for Motion & Orientation access.

• Inverted tilt? Enable "Flip Tilt Direction" on the home screen.

• Buttons / Keyboard: Tap the left/right screen edges or use ↓ / → for Correct and ↑ / ← for Pass.
